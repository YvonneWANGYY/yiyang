About

From simulation to inference.

I am a graduate student in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at Seoul National University, supported by the Global Korea Scholarship (GKS). I work with Prof. Ji-hoon Kim on computational astrophysics and machine learning for astrophysical simulations.

My current project develops a machine-only calibration framework using normalizing flows. I am especially interested in research that combines physically rich large-scale simulations with modern statistical and machine-learning methods.

Before SNU, I studied Physics at Shenzhen University with a minor in Computer Science, and spent an exchange year in Physics at the University of Seoul. My earlier computational work included condensed matter physics, nano-optics, and scientific programming.

Current research

M.S. research · Seoul National University

Machine-only calibration for astrophysical simulations

I am developing a calibration framework based on normalizing flows to infer and constrain astrophysical simulation parameters directly from simulation outputs. The broader goal is to build flexible machine-learning tools that can connect high-dimensional numerical models with physically meaningful observables and parameter constraints.
